Best-Sellers / Product RailLead with proven winners — "best sellers" is social proof that lowers decision risk and pulls shoppers into product views.
  • Product image (multi-shot carousel) — more angles reduce uncertainty and raise add-to-cart intent.
  • Colour swatches — preview and switch variants right on the card, so shoppers see "their" version without opening the PDP.
  • Wishlist / save (♡) — captures shoppers who like it but aren't ready to buy; the saved item becomes a re-marketing trigger (back-in-stock, price-drop) that brings them back, lifting eventual conversion.
  • Name + price with strike-through — instant value read; the anchor price makes the discount feel concrete.
  • Star rating — at-a-glance social proof that filters hesitation before the click.
  • Dual CTAs — Add to cart + Buy now — serve both deliberate and impulse buyers; Buy-now shortens the path to purchase.
  • Variant-selection modal on add — most items need a size/colour, so Add-to-cart opens a quick popup to choose. It's needed because adding without a variant would force a PDP detour or an error; it helps by letting shoppers configure and add from the rail itself — no page change — which is the single biggest driver of the card add-to-cart rate.
  • Sparing badges (Sale / New / Low stock) — scarcity and freshness cues nudge action without crying wolf.
